About the project

Understanding youth unemployment is the first step towards change.

Youth unemployment happens when young people who are available and seeking work cannot find suitable jobs. It can be influenced by skill gaps, limited experience, unequal access to opportunities and changes in the job market.

◎

The challenge

Many young people face difficulty finding their first job or matching their education with available roles.

↗

Our approach

Connect career information, skill-building resources and job-search guidance in one accessible place.

✦

Our vision

A future where every young person can access useful guidance and make informed career decisions.

Skill development

Build skills that help you move forward.

Choose a starting point, practise consistently and create small projects that demonstrate what you can do.

⌘

Digital & technical skills

Start with computer basics, programming fundamentals, web development, spreadsheets or data literacy.

✎

Communication skills

Practise clear writing, speaking, teamwork, active listening and professional email etiquette.

▣

Workplace readiness

Learn time management, problem-solving, collaboration and how to present your work in a portfolio.

Career guidance

A simple plan for your next step.

You do not need to figure everything out at once. Start with one practical action.

Know your strengths

List your interests, subjects you enjoy and skills you already have.

Prepare a clear resume

Include education, projects, skills and relevant activities honestly.

Practise interviews

Prepare a short introduction and practise explaining your projects.

Keep learning

Apply for suitable opportunities and improve from feedback.

Resume checklist

✓ Correct contact details
✓ Short career objective
✓ Education and projects
✓ Relevant skills
✓ Clear formatting
✓ No false claims


Interview tip

Use a real example to explain how you solved a problem, worked with others or learned something new.

Remember

Your first role does not define your whole career. Be curious, keep practising and seek guidance from trusted teachers, mentors and career services.
